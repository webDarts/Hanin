01 / About

My Name Is Hanin

A Visual Designer based in Amman, passionate about both product and graphic design, believing that visual thinking and functionality are inseparable.

For me, great design lies in the balance between aesthetics and purpose, creating solutions that are not only visually compelling but also meaningful and practical. I am currently studying Design and Visual Communication, exploring its diverse creative streams. Among these, I am most drawn to the Product Design stream, where functionality meets aesthetics to create impactful solutions.

My goal is to adopt this direction and develop designs that enhance everyday experiences through innovation and thoughtful craftsmanship.

Selected Projects

01
Fashion

Nubian-Inspired Fashion Collection

GJU Fashion Show

Hide Details

This collection draws its raw energy from the Nubians of Aswan, Egypt where tradition meets fearless expression. Inspired by their deep connection with animals, the audacity of keeping crocodiles as house guests, and the hypnotic rhythm of their dances and weddings, every piece pulses with life. The bold, sun-drenched colors and striking triangular architecture shape the designs, capturing the spirit of a culture that thrives in vibrancy and movement.

02
Fashion / Sustainability

Tide Piece

The Space of Fashion x Talabat Competition

View Details

For the Space Fashion x Talabat competition, I created a runway piece designed to be worn multiple times while embodying a strong sustainable message. The challenge required using second-hand fabrics and unconventional materials under a specific concept, with a focus on upcycling. All handmade by me, I collaborated with Project Sea to collect discarded plastic from the Red Sea, transforming waste into art. The skirt was crafted from old fabric, while the top was inspired by the shimmer of seashells — made by melting recovered ocean plastic and coating it with resin for a wet, oceanic effect. This piece celebrates both fashion innovation and environmental responsibility.

07
Product Design

Self-Watering Terracotta Planter

Ezzah

View Details

Handcrafted from premium Italian terracotta clay, this self-watering planter features a square outer form with a central cylindrical chamber that holds the plant. The surrounding space serves as a water reservoir. Through the natural porosity of terracotta, moisture is gradually released from the reservoir into the soil, enabling the plant to absorb only the amount of water it requires, while excess water remains stored for later use. This sustainable design ensures consistent hydration and reduces the need for frequent maintenance.
